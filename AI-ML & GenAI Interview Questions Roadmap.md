# 🏆 Interview Preparation Strategy

Work through the roadmap in order:

```text
Fundamentals
     ↓
Core ML Concepts
     ↓
ML Algorithms
     ↓
Deep Learning
     ↓
NLP
     ↓
Transformers & GPT
     ↓
Generative AI
     ↓
RAG
     ↓
System Design
     ↓
Evaluation & Production
     ↓
Advanced GenAI
     ↓
Real-World Scenarios
```

### ✅ Recommended Approach

For every question, try to prepare **three levels of answers**:

1. **🟢 Short Answer** — Explain it in 20–30 seconds.
2. **🟡 Technical Answer** — Explain the concept with technical details.
3. **🔴 Interview Answer** — Explain *why, when, and where* you would use it, preferably with an example.

> 💡 **Tip:** Don't memorize answers. Focus on understanding the concept well enough to explain it naturally and apply it to a real-world problem.
